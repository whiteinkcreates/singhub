## Coordinate reconciliation

`map-geocoding-audit-2026-11-06.csv` compares all 103 San Diego County venue addresses with OpenStreetMap Nominatim location candidates. **A geocoder match is not proof.** Identically numbered addresses in other cities can produce large false discrepancies. Six such suspect geocodes are explicitly rejected in the audit and were **not** applied.

This branch **stages**, but does **not** merge/deploy, coordinate corrections for BLVD, Carriage House, Hearth House, Jin Music Studios, Joycee's Cocktails, Kaminski's Sports Lounge, The Luau, El Viejon Seafood (Mira Mesa), and the previously missing Katy's Diner coordinates. Matching city/street/postal context was considered for corrections. Map fallbacks were aligned where those old entries existed.

These edits need independent map pin inspection and street-address verification before merge. **Carriage House and Jin Music Studios share one street address and now share one physical pin position.** The current Leaflet map may stack markers there. Fix marker access using a grouped popup or spiderfy, not invented offsets.

## Food candidate search

`scout-food-candidates-2026-11-06.csv` contains **309 slots (three per venue) across 103 San Diego County venues**, with blank slots intentionally maintained for gaps.

- **87 venues** currently have three OpenStreetMap candidate businesses within a **0.59-mile straight-line search radius**.
- **16 venues** have fewer than three; **six have zero candidates** in the OSM pass (Camels Bar & Grill, Don's Cocktails, BNS Brewing, Casino Inn, Casa Reveles, and Alpine VFW).
- **54 venues** have at least one candidate with an OSM opening-hours tag suggesting service until midnight or later on some day. **49 do not**.
- For nine venues, candidates were rerun against the corrected coordinates or newly geocoded Katy's Diner location.
- All 309 slots are marked **not publishable**: location and current existence need independent verification, direct geographic distance is not verified walking route distance, hour tags are not current reliable opening-hour confirmation, and food service timing must match the karaoke night.
- A food business at or near a venue may already supply its food, and a bar may prohibit outside food. Ask venue management before recommending takeout or merchant offers as an official partnership.
- Prioritize **no-kitchen bars**, local vendors and genuine walking connections. Where fewer than three usable businesses exist or late food is unavailable, **show fewer options rather than inventing them**.
- Different coffee/food/takeout categories may be useful for pre-mic vs post-mic, but confirm service hours and ordering availability independently.
- Food trucks are mobile: a street address alone cannot validate that a truck will be on-site at the specified karaoke time.
