# SingHUB Phase 1.5 Decisions

## Product language

- 📡 On the Radar
- 📍 SingHUB Has Been Here
- ✅ Verified Karaoke
- 🫡 SingHUB Salute
- 🎩 MadHatter

The public UI uses recognizable symbols inside custom SingHUB-colored badges. The symbols remain easy for the community to repeat in posts and messages, while the badge shape, color, border, typography, and glow create the owned visual system.

## Independent meanings

- A venue may show multiple badges.
- SingHUB Has Been Here is a recent firsthand confidence signal from MadHatter or another named human Scout.
- Verified Karaoke means the current event evidence is strong enough for schedule use.
- SingHUB Salute is an achievement awarded to a venue and/or specific karaoke event. It is not the same thing as a visit or verification.
- Singers Say is the public human-experience section on a venue profile.

## Market focus

San Diego remains the operating focus and the laboratory for AI Scout, human verification, community participation, and a repeatable city-launch process.

Boston is an external Scout pilot, beginning around Wild Rover. It is not presented as an official city launch.

## Community scope

The first room is San Diego. The initial channels are:

- tonight
- new-finds
- schedule-checks
- scout-ops

The first implementation is a mobile interaction prototype. Local preview posts are intentionally not persisted until accounts, moderation, and database rules are implemented.
